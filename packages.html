Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time?

Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or pause your package at the end of any monthly billing cycle with just 7 days notice to your dedicated account lead.

No, ad spend is paid directly to Meta, Google, or TikTok from your ad manager account. Our package fee covers complete campaign strategy, ad creative design, copywriting, media buying, and daily optimization.

Graphic assets and store optimizations are completed within the first 5-7 business days. Paid ad testing begins immediately, with initial ROAS signals and audience data typically stabilizing within 10-14 days.

Absolutely! We manage campaigns for brands across the US, UK, UAE, and APAC. We offer invoicing in USD, AED, or EUR with dedicated global account directors.
