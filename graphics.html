HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our graphic design workflows, IP ownership, and file deliverables.

Do I own 100% full commercial copyright and trademark rights?

Yes, absolutely. Upon project completion and final milestone approval, 100% intellectual property, trademark, and commercial distribution rights are transferred directly to your business.

Which file formats are provided upon project completion?

You receive full industry-standard production suites: Vector master files (Adobe Illustrator .AI, .EPS, .SVG, .PDF), high-resolution web formats (4K PNG, JPG, WebP), editable Figma/PSD templates, and 3D CAD files (.OBJ, .GLTF, .FBX).

How many revision rounds are included in my project?

All our packages include iterative refinement rounds during active design sprints to guarantee that the final logo, 3D packaging, and visual assets exceed your highest standards.

Are your packaging and box dielines ready for factory printing?

Yes. We build custom CMYK print dielines with millimeter-exact trim/bleed margins, barcode placement, foil stamping layers, spot UV masks, and embossing vectors compatible with global packaging manufacturers.
